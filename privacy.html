Privacy Policy

Smart Money Manager

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Money Manager handles information when you use our personal finance application.

2. Information We Handle

The application may store financial records that you enter, including income, expenses, transaction dates, categories, account information and notes.

Biometric authentication is handled by your Android device. The application does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric data.

3. Local Storage

Your financial records are stored locally on your device to provide the application's features.

4. Google Drive Backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the application uses Google's authorization system to access the files it is permitted to manage for backup and synchronization.

Backup files may contain financial records you have entered into the application. These files are stored in your Google Drive account.

Google authentication and data access are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy and terms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal financial information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except as necessary to provide features you choose to use, such as Google Drive backup.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect your information through device security, access controls and the security measures provided by Google services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can choose whether to connect Google Drive. You can also manage Google account permissions through your Google Account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for general personal finance management and is not specifically directed at children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
