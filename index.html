Welcome to Smart Money Manager

Smart Money Manager is a personal finance application designed to help you manage your income, expenses, accounts and daily financial activities in one place.

Features

  • Track income and expenses.
  • Organize transactions by categories.
  • View daily financial summaries and statistics.
  • Edit and manage transaction history.
  • Secure access with biometric protection.
  • Google Drive backup and synchronization.
  • Green and dark themes.
